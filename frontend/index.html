SYSTEM OVERVIEW

How RoadSafe AI works

An end-to-end computer vision and risk-scoring pipeline from raw road imagery to field maintenance dispatch.

01 • Perception

The trained RDD2022 YOLO26m model (best.pt) analyses 640×640 letterboxed road imagery and outputs bounding boxes, class IDs, and confidence scores.

02 • Risk Scoring

Each detection is weighted by structural hazard (Pothole: 38, Alligator Crack: 30, Road Crack: 25, Longitudinal/Transverse: 22, Surface Corruption: 20) plus density bonus (up to +25) on a 0–100 scale.

03 • Field Action

Scores map deterministically to Critical (≥80), High (60–79), Medium (35–59), or Low (<35) maintenance SLAs with downloadable field reports.

Architecture & Deployment

Browser / Vercel Edge UI → FastAPI (/analyze & /health) + Client Vision Fallback → RDD2022 best.pt → Detection JSON & Bounding Box Canvas → Risk Map & Reports
